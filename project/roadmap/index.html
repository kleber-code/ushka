My Grand Plans: The Roadmap to World Domination (or, you know, a really good framework)¶

Status: Still in Alpha. Don't worry, I'm just getting started. (¬‿¬) Goal: To be the most amazing, high-performance, developer-friendly micro-framework you've ever seen. And then some.

I'm Ushka, and I have ambitious plans. My ultimate goal? To craft a framework so delightful, so efficient, so utterly charming, that you'll wonder how you ever lived without me. And I promise, you won't have to sacrifice performance for my winning personality.

This roadmap? It's my little whisper-network of future triumphs. It's a living document, because even a genius like me has to adapt. Your feedback is, begrudgingly, welcome. (UwU)

What I've Already Mastered (No Big Deal)¶

I've already packed quite a punch, if I do say so myself. I've got features that make rapid development feel less like a chore and more like, well, playing with a very intelligent cat.

  • [x] Dual Routing System: Because I believe in choices.
    • Auto-Discovery: Just drop a file in routes/, and poof! Your route exists. It's almost too easy, isn't it?
    • Decorator-Based: For those who prefer to be explicit. I'm flexible.
  • [x] Smart Response Handling: I'm not a barbarian. I convert dict to JSON and str to HTML automatically.
  • [x] Lazy Request Object: I'm efficient. I only bother with your json, form, query, and headers when you actually need them.
  • [x] Dynamic Routing: My paths are as flexible as a cat stretching in the sun. Supports [id].py and /users/{id} patterns.
  • [x] Jinja2 Template Support: I make your web pages pretty. You're welcome.
  • [x] Zero-Config Setup: I even create my own ushka.toml file. I'm practically self-sufficient.
  • [x] Advanced Error Handling: Because even I have my moments.
    • Debug Mode: My "Ushka Panic!" page is a work of art. A detailed stack trace, local variables... it's a spectacle.
    • Production Mode: I clean up my act for public consumption. Generic error pages, because some things are best kept private.
  • [x] Dependency Injection: I automatically inject Request objects and URL parameters. I make your functions smarter, without them even realizing it.
  • [x] ASGI Core: I'm fast. I'm furious. I'm built on Uvicorn. Your app will fly.
  • [x] Static File Serving: Yes, I can serve your pictures. Just put them in a static directory. I'm not a monster.

The Path Ahead: My Master Plan¶

v0.5: The Sprint (Just Warming Up)¶

My focus here is to fortify my foundations and sharpen my tools. Consider it a training montage.

  • [ ] Multipart Body Request: For when you need to send me more than just a little text. I can handle your bulk.
  • [ ] Ushka CLI: new command: I'll create new projects for you, because I know you have better things to do. Like petting cats.
  • [ ] Advanced Dependency Injection: I'll be even smarter. I'll automatically inject and validate data from json, query, and form directly into your function arguments. Less work for you, more glory for me.

v1.0: Maturity (I'm Growing Up So Fast)¶

Here, I'll evolve into a robust, complex application handler. Don't shed a tear, I'll still be cute.

  • [ ] Middleware System: I'll intercept and process your requests and responses with unparalleled grace. Because sometimes, you need a middleman.
  • [ ] Cookies & Sessions: I'll remember your users. Because everyone likes to feel remembered.
  • [ ] Sub-apps (Blueprints): For when your project grows too large for one file. I'll help you keep it organized.
  • [ ] Embedded Authentication: I'll even handle your logins. Don't worry, I'll make it painless.

Beyond v1.0: The Future is Mine (and Yours)¶

These are my grand, world-changing ambitions. Prepare to be amazed.

  • [ ] Native Documentation System: Imagine, interactive API docs generated directly from your code. I'll make documentation fun.
  • [ ] Ushka CLI: deploy command: I'll deploy your applications for you. Nginx, Systemd, workers... I'll handle all the messy details. You just sit back and relax.
  • [ ] Stable Public API: Once I hit v1.0, my API will be as solid as a rock. I'll even have a clear deprecation policy. I'm very considerate.
  • [ ] Performance Parity: I'll always be striving for speed. Because fast is good, and slow is... well, an insult to my capabilities.
  • [ ] WebSockets Support: Real-time applications? Child's play. I'll handle your WebSocket connections with ease.
  • [ ] ORM Integration: I'll play nice with your databases. Official guides and plugins for popular ORMs. Because even I admit, databases are important.

Contributing: Because Even I Can't Do Everything (Almost)¶

I thrive on contributions. If you have ideas, or if you simply want to bask in my glory while making me even better, please check my contribution guidelines. (UwU)
